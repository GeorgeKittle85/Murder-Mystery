SuddenDeath

A 3D murder mystery. You are Detective Eighty-Five: former tight end, current truth-seeker. You block for the innocent and you catch the guilty.

How to play

Walk the facility, inspect anything that sparkles, and talk to everyone still locked inside. Evidence and statements go into your Playbook. When you know the answer, open the Playbook and make the call.

Four downs

  • Every wrong accusation burns a down. Miss on fourth down and it's a turnover: the killer walks.
  • Each accusation tells you how many parts you got right.
  • You get three timeouts per case. Each one buys a hint and costs a few rating points.

Detective work

  • Suspects with a gold ! over their head have something new to tell you, usually because of evidence you found.
  • In Overtime, watch hands and shoes. Physical clues give you the killer's shoe size, dominant hand and badge class, and only one suspect fits all three.
  • Five golden footballs are hidden around the building. They don't solve anything. Find them anyway.
